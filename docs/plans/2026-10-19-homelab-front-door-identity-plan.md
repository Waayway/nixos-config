# Homelab front door: plan for logins (Kanidm)

What this part delivers: one login for the homelab. A new small VM, **identity (111)**,
runs Kanidm at **`id.vanwaaij.com`**. People sign in there once, with a passkey or a
password plus an authenticator app, and the apps trust that login.

In this part:

- apps **without** their own login support get a login screen in front of them on the
  router (the "gate"): Archipelago, the Traefik dashboard, TrueNAS and Backrest;
- **Proxmox** gets "sign in with Kanidm" next to its normal login.

Lodestar, Nextcloud, Jellyfin and Immich switch to Kanidm in their own later plans
(Lodestar needs code changes first; the other three move to the new production VM).

Design: `2026-10-19-homelab-front-door-design.md` (section D). Technical version for the
implementing agents: `2026-10-19-homelab-front-door-identity-plan-technical.md`.
Starts after the router plan is done.

## Who does what

- **Claude** does the repo work, the VMs on `pve`, the Proxmox settings and testing.
- **You:**
  1. Give Claude the list of people who should get an account (name, e-mail) and which

  2. Create one Tailscale Service, `id`, in the admin console (step 5). Claude gives you

  3. Set up your own passkey with the link Claude sends you (step 6), and pass the

- Nothing gets pushed to GitHub without your OK.

## Step 1: Who gets access to what

Claude adds two things to the repo:

- **A list of people**, once: name and e-mail, and whether someone is an admin.
- **Per app, who may use it**, written next to that app's settings. For example next to
  Archipelago: "thijs, and these friends". For the apps the repo doesn't manage yet
  (TrueNAS, Archipelago, Proxmox, Traefik, Backrest), this lives in one file for now
  and moves next to the app when it moves to NixOS.

Kanidm builds its groups from this: each app gets a group (`archipelago-users`, …), and
the people listed become members. Giving someone access later means adding a name and
rebuilding.

**You:** send Claude the list of people and which apps each may use. Until you do,
the list contains only you.

## Step 2: Describing the identity VM

Claude writes the configuration for VM **111** (1 core, 2 GB memory):

- **Kanidm** at `id.vanwaaij.com`, with its own certificate (requested through
  Cloudflare, like the router's).
- **Two-factor for everyone:** a password alone is never enough. Admins must use a
  passkey; everyone else may also use a password with an authenticator app.
- **No mail server.** New people and "I lost my phone" get a one-time link that Claude
  (or you) generates and you send yourself.
- **Admin passwords and app secrets** are generated by Claude and stored in 1Password
  (vault Homelab); the VM fetches them from there. Nothing secret goes into the repo.
- **Daily backup** of Kanidm's data to TrueNAS (`/store/kanidm`), which the existing
  nightly backup to Hetzner already picks up.

And on the router:

- `id.vanwaaij.com` is passed straight through to VM 111. The router doesn't open the
  encrypted traffic, so even the router never sees passwords.
- **The gate:** a small login checker (oauth2-proxy) next to Traefik. When a website on
  the list is marked "gated", a visitor without a valid Kanidm login is sent to
  `id.vanwaaij.com` first, and only gets through if they're in that app's group. One
  login works for all gated sites.
- **New routes** for the gated apps that don't have one yet: `truenas.vanwaaij.com` and
  `backrest.vanwaaij.com`, **Tailscale only**. Archipelago's old username and password
  are replaced by the gate.

Before anything is installed, Claude checks that the configuration produces exactly
this and that all other machines still build.

## Step 3: Trying it on the test VM (230)

VM 230 (the router's test VM) also gets a test Kanidm at `id-test.vanwaaij.com`. Claude
checks:

- Kanidm starts and contains exactly the groups and app connections from the repo, and
  the two-factor rule is switched on;
- the echo test page is behind the gate: without a login it sends you to the test
  Kanidm; a faked "I'm logged in" header doesn't get you through; the login can't be
  abused to redirect people to a site outside `vanwaaij.com`;
- sites that aren't gated (Jellyfin) still work without a login;
- a backup file appears on TrueNAS.

Actually signing in is first tried with your own account in step 7.

**You notice:** nothing.

## Step 4: The real identity VM (111)

Claude creates VM **111** from the template, starts it with the identity configuration,
waits for its certificate and checks that `https://id.vanwaaij.com` shows the Kanidm
login page, from the internet and from the LAN.

## Step 5: Tailscale (you)

Create the Service `id` in the Tailscale admin console and add the tag `tag:identity`
to the policy (Claude gives you the exact text).
Claude then adds its address to the router so `id.vanwaaij.com` also works over
Tailscale, and checks it.

## Step 6: Your account

Claude generates a one-time link for your account. **You** open it and set up a passkey
(your Mac, phone or security key). Claude generates the links for the other people on
the list; you send them on. Each person sets up their own passkey or password plus
authenticator app.

## Step 7: Turning on the gate

Claude marks Archipelago, Traefik, TrueNAS and Backrest as gated and rebuilds the
router. Then checks with your account:

- Archipelago asks for the Kanidm login instead of the old password, and works after
  login;
- the Traefik dashboard, TrueNAS and Backrest open after the same login, over
  Tailscale;
- someone who isn't in a group gets "access denied".

TrueNAS and Backrest keep their own login behind the gate, and are still reachable
directly on the LAN as before; the gate adds a lock in front, it doesn't remove
anything.

**If anything fails:** the gate is switched off again for that site with one change,
and Archipelago gets its old password back.

## Step 8: Proxmox

Claude adds "Kanidm" as a sign-in option in Proxmox. People in the `admins` group become
Proxmox administrators. The normal `root` login stays as the emergency way in.

**You:** try signing in to Proxmox with Kanidm once.

## Step 9: Paperwork

Claude updates the docs: the homelab inventory (VM 111, the gate, which apps use
Kanidm), how to add a person or give someone access, how to make a recovery link, and
where the emergency logins are.

## When this part is done

- `id.vanwaaij.com` is the one login for the homelab, with two-factor for everyone.
- Archipelago, Traefik, TrueNAS and Backrest are behind that login; Proxmox can use it.
- Giving someone access is adding a name in the repo and rebuilding.
- Next: Lodestar on its own URL with this login (plan 3).
